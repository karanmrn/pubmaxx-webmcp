## The _best pub gardens_ in _South East London_

The sun’s out (finally), the pints are cold, and South East London’s best pub gardens are calling your name. Whether you’re riverside in Greenwich, tucked away in a Dulwich courtyard, or soaking up the buzz in Peckham, there’s a spot waiting for you.

Think ice-cold pints cheering in the sun, Burger Shacks sizzling away, and long, lazy afternoons stretching into golden evenings. There’s always _that one mate_ claiming the best seat, a dog snoozing under the table, and a group debating who is the GOAT, like it’s life or death.

To make summer even easier, we’ve rounded up the best pub gardens in South East London (we know, we’re good to you). So grab your sunnies, call your mates, and let’s make this a summer to remember.
