## The Best _Pub Gardens_ in _East London_

The moment the sun makes an appearance, rooftops, courtyards, and beer gardens come alive with buzzing energy, ice-cold pints, and that unbeatable Friday feeling (even on a Tuesday).

Whether you’re soaking up the sun in a Hackney beer garden, sipping spritzes on a rooftop in Shoreditch, or stretching out in Bethnal Green, East London’s pub gardens are _the_ place to be. Think juicy burgers fresh off the grill, crisp Rosé flowing, and that golden-hour glow making everything feel just right.

We’ve rounded up the best spots for lazy afternoons, long summer nights, and spontaneous “just one more” moments.
