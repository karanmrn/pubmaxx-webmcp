## The _best pub gardens_ in _Central London_

This is all about ice-cold pints, sun-drenched terraces, and that unbeatable golden-hour glow over the city skyline. Young’s pub gardens in Central London are _the_ place to be, whether you’re sipping spritzes on a rooftop, soaking up riverside views of the Thames, or finding a hidden courtyard right in the heart of the action.

From lazy afternoons that turn into lively evenings to spontaneous post-work pints that somehow last until closing, our pub gardens are ready for it all.

## Post work drinks

The clock hits five (or, let’s be honest, four-thirty on a sunny day), and there’s only one thing on your mind—grabbing a drink in one of Central London’s best pubs. Whether you’re escaping the office, celebrating a win, or just in need of a well-earned pint, the pub is the perfect spot.

From buzzing rooftop terraces with skyline views to riverside pubs along the Thames, our pubs are made for those golden-hour gatherings.
