## The Best _Pub Gardens_ in _West London_

When summer hits West London, pub gardens turn into the ultimate sun-soaked playgrounds!

Whether you’re looking to sip a chilled pint in Hammersmith, bask in the sunshine in Shepherd’s Bush, or lounge in style in Isleworth, our pub gardens are the only place to be. Think chilled drinks, sunshine, and that laid-back vibe you only get when the sun’s out and the beer’s flowing.

Grab your mates, find the perfect spot under the sun (or shade if you’re feeling fancy), and let the good times roll. From afternoon G&Ts to sunset spritzes, we’ve got the perfect setting for every summer vibe.
