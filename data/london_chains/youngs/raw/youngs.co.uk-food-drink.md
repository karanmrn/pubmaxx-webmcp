## The Best _Seasonal_ British _Food_

As spring unfolds into summer and the days grow longer, our kitchens are celebrating the season the best way we know how, with fresh flavours, vibrant dishes, and the very best British produce. Over the coming months, you’ll spot beautiful new additions to our menus, each one inspired by the fields, farms, and coastlines of Britain in full bloom.

Think bright, seasonal ingredients, lighter plates, and a few indulgent twists along the way, all crafted with care, sustainability, and seasonality at heart. From sun-ripened vegetables to fresh coastal catches, every dish is a taste of the season.

# Food Made Good

Once again we’re proud to have achieved a top three-star rating in the 2026 Food Made Good Standard by The Sustainable Restaurant Association, recognising our commitment to sustainability across sourcing, community impact and environmental responsibility.

From locally sourced seasonal ingredients and sustainably sourced seafood to reducing food waste and rolling out solar energy across our pubs, we’re continuing to take positive steps towards a more sustainable future.

## A True _London_ Original

With 193 years in industry, it’s safe to say that when it comes to great beer we know a thing or two. So, we decided it was time for a change, and paying homage to our rich brewing heritage we launched our shiny new pump clips and \*brewtiful\* glasses across our pubs.

After months of deliberation (mainly in the pub of course), we finally decided we wanted to bring our designs back to our London roots, proud that we have been a London brand since 1831. Now of course we absolutely LOVE our new designs, but what better way to find out what our pubs thought than to pay them a visit.
