## The _best pub gardens_ in _South West London_

When the sun’s out, there’s nowhere better than a pub garden. Whether you’re riverside in Richmond, soaking up the vibes in Clapham, or in a Wandsworth beer garden, it’s an unbeatable British summer feeling.

A crisp pint in hand, your mate trying (and failing) to secure the best suntrap seat, and the sound of ice clinking in a G&T. The Burger Shack is sizzling, the Rosé is flowing, and someone’s already claiming “ _this is the life_”—because, honestly, it is.

From sun-drenched terraces to hidden garden gems, South West London has some of the best pub gardens in the world, _yes, we said it_. So grab your sunnies, round up the crew, and get yourself to a pub garden—because summer is meant to be spent here.
