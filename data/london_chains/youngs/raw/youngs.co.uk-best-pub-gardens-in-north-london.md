## The _Best_ Pub Gardens in _North London_

There is no better feeling than basking in a pub garden during the glorious summer months. And if you’re in North London, you’re spoilt for choice when it comes to sun-drenched spots perfect for sipping, snacking, and soaking up the vibes.

From riverside pints in Angel to leafy escapes near Hampstead Heath, North London’s pub gardens are the place to be when the sun’s out. An ice-cold G&T in hand, the smell of a Burger Shack wafting through the air, and that golden-hour glow making everything feel just special. Whether you’re gathering the gang for a lazy afternoon session or chasing the last rays of sunshine with a spritz in hand, we’ve got you covered.

To make sure you hit the best spots, we’ve put together the ultimate guide to North London’s top summer pub gardens. You’re welcome.
