## Discover the best

## _pub gardens_

Pub gardens in summer? Oh yes please, they’re absolutely unbeatable! The sun beaming down, all you can see are smiles and the sound of _who’s getting the next round?_

You’re stretched out, sunglasses on, sipping a crisp pint or a refreshing spritz, doesn’t matter as long as it cold and there’s a lazy, golden glow over everything. Summer hits differently, whether it’s a long afternoon with friends, a spontaneous evening session, or a Sunday roast under blue skies, there’s nothing quite like the relaxed, sun-soaked bliss of a summer pub garden.
