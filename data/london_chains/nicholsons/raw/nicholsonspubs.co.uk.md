# NICHOLSON’S: A COLLECTION OF GREAT HISTORIC BRITISH PUBS

Since 1873 when we opened our doors, we’ve been delighting guests with pubs of distinction, delicious food, perfectly paired drinks, and so much more. Whether you’re looking for classic British pub dishes or seasonal dishes, Nicholson’s is proud to serve alongside an unrivalled collection of casks ales and an ever-growing range of premium gins.
