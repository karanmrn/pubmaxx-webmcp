Seven concepts, three directions, one recommendation. All marks shown at 64 / 32 / 16 on paper and ink. 2026-07-21, Fable + three design forks.

Direction 1: Pub craft

The Clink Recommended

Two pints the second they touch in a toast. The ember is the clink.

The X is two pints the second they touch in a toast, the moment a night officially starts. The ember dot in the middle is the clink itself. Wide mouths up, narrow bases down: the taper reads as glasses at size and collapses to a confident chiselled X at 16px.

Watch at production: taper must stay modest or it drifts bowtie small.

Crossed keys are the oldest promise on a pub sign: the landlord keeps the keys to the good room. These unlock the whole city's nights.

Handsome, historic, cold. Heraldic borrow carries church residue; least joy of the seven.

Direction 3: Living system

The Signature Strike

Hand-struck calligraphic beams, 1.75:1 weight ratio, ember kept at the crossing.

96 paper
96 ink

Two strokes struck by hand, never perfectly even, because nights out never are. Comes with the full system: the Strike draw animation (420ms, beams then ember pop), the seal stamp for completed nights, the bare-X map pin, the breathing-node loading state.

Mark alone is quieter than the Clink; its power is the system, which transplants onto any winner.

Verdict

Ship the Clink as the master mark; adopt the Strike system around it.

The Clink is the only mark where the joy is structural: it is literally the moment the product exists for, two pints touching as the night starts, and the ember finally has a reason to be there (it is the clink). It keeps continuity with the current mark's ember so the brand evolves rather than reboots, it carries flat-cut chiselled terminals for the sharpness the 𝕏 reference asks for, and it survives 16px with the node dropped (the #444 small-optics precedent).

From the system direction, transplant: the Strike draw-in (heavy beam 180ms, second beam overlapping, ember pop at the clink moment, reduced-motion fade), the completed-night seal (the Clink stamped at minus 8 degrees in an broken ink ring; every confirmed night mints one on the recap and the profile collects them), the bare-mark map pin ("X marks tonight"), and the breathing-ember loading state.

Runner-up preserved: Ember Cut, if a colder, bolder letterform is preferred. Chalk Strike deliberately not shipped despite being sharpest: closest confusion risk with X/Twitter itself, the one thing this brand must never borrow.

Owner decisions on pick-up: choose master mark (Clink recommended), then one activation lane cuts favicon/ICO/touch icons/store set/OG stamps from the single geometry source (gen-brand-assets.mjs pattern, MARK_GEOMETRY update, PubmaxxMark component), and the Strike lands with the fontPartyContainment-style discipline: one component family, reduced-motion honoured.
