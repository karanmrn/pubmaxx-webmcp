### Why each column passes

**Reduced motion - ✅ (verified, not assumed).** The catch-all in
`app/globals.css` neutralises CSS animation and transitions. The internal spring
controller in `lib/useSpringValue.ts` reads the media query directly, jumps an
active or newly requested spring to its target, and cancels its frame. Phone
sheet, tablet drawer, and desktop drawer consumers therefore share the same
reduced-motion result. The WebGL map also reads the preference directly for
explicit camera and pin motion.

**Focus management - ✅.** `MobileSharedSheet.tsx` captures and restores the
opener, focuses its close control, closes on Escape, and traps focus at the half
and full detents while the background is inert. `CommandPalette.tsx` and
`FirstRunTour.tsx` own the same modal guarantees. The desktop venue drawer
reuses `useFocusTrap` for its full open lifetime; Escape restores the keyboard
opener after selection-history settlement. `IdentityNudge.tsx` and
`AccountOnboarding.tsx` use the strict modal policy: focus starts on the named
dialog, all outside chrome becomes inert, Tab stays inside, and close restores
the prior focus target. Map sheets use the separate map-surface policy so their
primary navigation and onboarding sibling can keep their existing interaction
contract.

**Touch ≥44px — ✅.** Tab bar holds `min-height:44px` down to the smallest
phone; venue-sheet tabs, actions, and citation chips use the same target floor.
`expectTouchTargets()` in `e2e/mobile-map-shell-matrix.spec.ts` enforces it
across the viewport and theme matrix.

**aria / landmark — ✅ / 🔧.** Tab bar = `<nav aria-label="Primary">` with
`aria-current="page"` on the active link and hidden decorative icons. Map region
has an accessible name. Skeletons expose status updates through `aria-live`.

**Keyboard: ✅.** `MapVenueList` exposes native venue buttons for the exact
filtered venues projected inside the rendered viewport. While open, membership
tracks camera movement; UK base rows also respect the base-layer zoom floor.
Rows use the same selection handlers as pins without rendered-feature queries
or canvas hit-testing. On phones, the planner action yields while the list owns
the map and returns when the list closes, so no hidden control can intercept a
row tap. Search, the tonight lane, `/tonight`, and `/feed` remain additional
discovery paths.
