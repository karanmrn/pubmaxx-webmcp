# East London pub-scene sweep — last 30 days (to 18 Jul 2026)

**Researcher:** East London lane. **Window:** facts dated 19 Jun–18 Jul 2026 (inner areas); 19 May–18 Jul (outer/quiet areas, ≤60 days as briefed).
**Sourcing:** All facts carry a source URL + publish date. Data gathered via **Exa API** (healthy). See "API status" at foot — **Firecrawl was DOWN (HTTP 401) for the entire sweep**; this brief is Exa-only.

> Note on prices: hard *named-pub per-pint* prices are genuinely scarce in the last-30-day press for East London (most coverage quotes spend-bands or citywide averages). ## Shoreditch / Hoxton / Brick Lane
- **Kong — "Chinese public house"** taking over the derelict **Water Poet, Folgate St** (closed since 2019; 6,000 sq ft + 800 sq ft courtyard). Backed by **Andrew Wong** (2-Michelin A Wong) + **Amar Radia** (Dishoom co-founder). Opening later in 2026. (timeout.com, 2026-06-26; thedrinksbusiness.com, 2026-07-01)
- **The Little Neon Door** — Little Door & Co's **6th and largest** venue, **Great Eastern St**, opened week of 7–8 Jul; two floors of "house-party" themed rooms, DJ sets, artist-designed entrance; a 50% off launch offer ran. (londonxlondon.com, 2026-07-07; theupcoming.co.uk, 2026-07-08)
- **Roxy Ball Room Old Street** opened **Thu 16 Jul** at **241 Old St**, ~13,500 sq ft "Booze & Ball Games" (duckpin bowling, pool, tech darts, karaoke). Second Roxy in the capital. (hospitalityandcateringnews.com, 2026-07-17)
- **MITSU** — Japanese izakaya/nightlife venue on **Willow St**, opened Apr 2026, East-Tokyo-inspired. (premierconstructionnews.com, 2026-06-19)
- **Tasca** (Portuguese/Spanish) summer pop-up took over **Project 44, Rivington St** as an evening wine bar — a rerun of its Bethnal Green residency; note its previous host **Câv (Bethnal Green) has since closed**. (londontheinside.com, 2026-07-14)

## Product implications (for PUBMAXX surfacing)
1. **"Just opened / reopening" freshness rail for East London.** Concrete, dateable openings this month: Roxy Ball Room Old St (16 Jul), Hon's BBQ Hackney Wick (17 Jul), Rocola Mare St (8 Jul), Little Neon Door Shoreditch (7 Jul), Mary Wollstonecraft Stoke Newington (10 Jul). A "New this month" module with open-date + one-line hook would be immediately populatable.
2. **Coming-soon / countdown tiles.** Kong (Shoreditch), The Tallyman (Canary Wharf), Vagabond self-pour (Wood Wharf, Aug) are all pre-open with strong hooks — a "watch this space" surface builds anticipation and repeat visits.
3. **Closure/at-risk flags to keep data honest.** Coupette (shut), White Hart Stoke Newington (locked out), Ye Olde Rose & Crown (auctioned), Durham Arms (planning breach) — a lightweight "status: closed/uncertain" flag prevents sending users to dead venues and is a trust signal.
4. **Own the pint-price gap.** Press has citywide averages (£5.34, "£10 pint") but almost no *named East-London pub* prices. A crowd-sourced / harvested **"what's a pint here" field per venue** would be genuinely differentiated data nobody else surfaces well. Seed with the few knowns (Dalston Superstore £5).
5. **"Cheap pint / value" filter.** With the £10-pint narrative dominating, a **value lens** (£5-and-under pints, happy hours, £5 margaritas) is on-trend and maps to real signals (Dalston Superstore, Star of Bethnal Green, Wetherspoon sites).
6. **Blackhorse Beer Mile & brewery-taproom cluster as a "trail."** Walthamstow (Big Penny Social, Signature Brew) and Hackney Wick (Howling Hops, Five Points, Crate) support a **beer-mile / taproom-crawl route** feature — high on the "unsung areas" the owner wants covered.
7. **Awards badge.** Surface **CAMRA Pub Design Award 2026** on Leyton Engineer + The European, and Time Out "Best in Hackney"-type claims, as trust/quality badges on venue cards.
8. **Residency & pop-up layer.** Eat Momo @ Coach and Horses (Leyton), Jurkish @ Ninety One (Brick Lane), Tasca @ Project 44 (Shoreditch) — time-boxed food residencies inside pubs are a recurring East pattern; a "limited-time pop-up" tag drives urgency.
9. **Live-sport / late-licence surfacing.** The World Cup showed strong demand for "who's screening + who's open late + walk-in vs booking." A **"showing the match / open late tonight"** real-time surface (Big Penny Social, Number 90, Star of Bethnal Green) is a proven footfall driver to template for future tournaments.
10. **Heritage / character angle for outer areas.** The Eastbrook (Dagenham), The Grapes (Limehouse, McKellen-owned), Saxon King smokehouse (Romford) give the unsung outer boroughs distinct hooks — a **"proper local"** editorial tag helps these compete with Shoreditch for attention.
