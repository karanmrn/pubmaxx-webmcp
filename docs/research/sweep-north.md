### Product implications

1. **Live "matchday surge pricing" alerts.** North London went from free entry to a £20 door charge (Clissold Park Tavern) for the same England game, and chains added 20p/pint during England matches. PUBMAXX could surface a per-pub "matchday entry fee / surge" flag so users aren't ambushed — genuinely differentiated, sourced from local reporting.
2. **Reopening tracker as a content engine.** In 30 days North London alone saw Camden Barfly, Mother Red Cap (Archway), Brendan the Navigator (Highgate), King William IV (Hampstead), The Queens (Crouch End), Black Horse (Barnet), Mary Wollstonecraft (Stoke Newington) all reopen/open. A "just reopened near you" feed is high-freshness, low-competition content.
3. **"Saved by locals / at-risk pub" badge.** Woodbine (flats refused), Alliance (save campaign), White Hart (locked out), Black Horse (rescued by 3 regulars), The George Harrow (up for lease), Station Tavern (lost to HMO). A community-status layer (thriving / at-risk / saved / lost) taps strong local emotion and CAMRA/ACV data.
4. **Price-band honesty by area, anchored to real data.** With London Asahi at £7.60 and Wetherspoon Murphy's at £2.99, the spread is enormous. Show a per-pub/per-area pint-price band benchmarked to the MA £5.34 UK average so users see "cheap for the area" vs "premium."
5. **£10-pint watch / "value pub" finder.** As the £10 pint becomes normal centrally, a filter for pubs still under a threshold (e.g. sub-£6) would be a killer North London feature — Wetherspoons, CAMRA real-ale stock, outer-zone locals.
6. **Award & recognition signals.** Lord Southampton (design award), Windsor Castle East Finchley (UK's best licensees), The Queens (£150k heritage refurb). An "award-winning / recently restored" badge helps quality-seekers and gives editorial hooks.
7. **Heritage/architecture layer.** Grade II listings (Pineapple, The Queens' Art Nouveau glass), 1720s Black Horse, ex-cinema Coronet. A "historic interior" filter differentiates from generic listings and suits the pub-crawl-guide content already being published (Hampstead route).
8. **Outer-zone opportunity feed.** Barnet, Harrow, Enfield, Wembley are underserved by pub media yet full of stories (Black Horse, The George lease, matchday Wembley). Treat outer North London as first-class — less competition, loyal locals, matchday spikes.
9. **Matchday/stadium mode.** Wembley matchday pub guides and Euston/Greene Man World Cup pushes show demand for "big game near a venue" discovery — pre-match pub finder with capacity/queue/entry-fee intel.
10. **Irish-pub / Guinness sub-scene.** Multiple Irish-flavoured stories (Mother Red Cap "great craic," Mannions Prince Arthur, Mary Wollstonecraft pointedly NOT serving Guinness, Guinness-to-£13 forecast). A "best Guinness / Irish pub" vertical has clear North London demand and a built-in price-anxiety narrative.
